THE KALKULAM FAMILY ATLAS

A story we share.

This living archive begins with Kalkulam T. Kumarapilla and Valliyamma Pilla Thankachi, their eleven children, and their families. The Arumanai branch of K. Vishwanathan Thampi now connects six recorded generations.

Double-ended arrows represent marriage. Descending lines connect parents to their children. Tap a name to see its relationships. Use + on a family to reveal its children in the tree, or focus on a branch to explore it at a readable scale.

Only supplied relationships appear here. A branch with no recorded descendants is an invitation to add its next chapter; it does not mean there were no children.

Names follow the supplied diagrams. Locations and notes are retained as recorded; dates and birth order are not yet known. Branch numbers are for navigation.
